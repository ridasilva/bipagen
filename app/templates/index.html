{{ _('About the Project') }}

{{ _('EMU Biological Collections Infrastructure: Acquisition of a Multi-User Platform for Microbiological Collections aimed at Bioprospecting, Pathogens and Genetic Models (BIPAGEN) is a project that proposes the creation of a multi-user platform for the storage, characterization, exchange, and safe use of microbiological collections encompassing microorganisms of interest in bioprospecting, clinical and veterinary pathogens, and genetic models for basic research.') }}

{{ _('The platform integrates high-standard physical infrastructure for microbial preservation, digital collection management systems, and biosafety protocols, serving as a national hub for academic collaboration and technology transfer. Brazil holds one of the largest microbial biodiversities on the planet, still under-explored. Many strains with potential for antibiotics, enzymes, biopolymers, or pharmaceutical and agricultural compounds remain restricted to isolated labs without unified management or structured sharing.') }}

{{ _('Main Goals') }}

  1. {{ _('Establish physical infrastructure for long-term microbial conservation (-80°C, lyophilization, cryopreservation).') }}
  2. {{ _('Create a computerized system for strain registration, traceability, and access.') }}
  3. {{ _('Implement biosafety protocols with BSL-1 and BSL-2 containment levels.') }}
  4. {{ _('Develop a database interconnected with national and international collection networks.') }}
  5. {{ _('Train users in safe handling and legal documentation of microbial resources.') }}
  6. {{ _('Promote open access for the scientific community and companies with a transparent usage policy.') }}
